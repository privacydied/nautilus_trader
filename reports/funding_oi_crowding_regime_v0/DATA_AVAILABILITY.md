### Why Binance REST openInterestHist is rejected

The Binance REST endpoint `openInterestHist` (`GET /futures/data/openInterestHist`) 
provides open-interest history for a single trailing month only. This design makes it
unsuitable as a study data source for the following reasons:

- **Trailing month only**: The endpoint returns at most 30 days of history with 5-minute
  granularity. It cannot serve multi-year archive coverage.
- **Cannot support 180-day past-only percentile warmup**: The 180-day backward-looking
  funding-percentile threshold computation requires OI history extending at least 180 days
  before the first evaluated funding event. A trailing-month API cannot provide this.
- **Cannot support the multi-year archive study**: The study requires coverage spanning
  at least 180 days of warmup, then 5,500+ aligned 8h funding settlement slots, reaching
  at least 2025-01-01. A trailing-month API covers at most ~93 settlement slots.
- **Not usable as fallback study data**: Even as a diagnostic fallback, the trailing-month
  window is too short to compute any meaningful 180-day percentile-based conditioning.

Therefore, `openInterestHist` is **rejected** as the study OI data source. The Binance
Vision daily public archive (`data/futures/um/daily/metrics/BTCUSDT/`) is the required
and sufficient source for this study.
